# **App Name**: ScholarMate AI

## Core Features:

- AI Quiz Generator: Generates various question types (MCQ, short, conceptual/scenario-based) with adjustable difficulty. For teachers, it creates quizzes based on syllabus with options to include/exclude specific topics or keywords, ensuring comprehensive topic coverage. For students, it generates timer-based quizzes relevant to their uploaded study materials (PDF, PPTX, DOCX), using the uploaded content as a tool to create questions based on user-selected criteria for question types, difficulty, and content focus.
- AI Structured Notes Generator: For students, processes attached PDFs or prompts to create structured notes (heading, subpoints, key ideas) with summary and detailed toggles. This tool will reason when to extract and include information.
- AI Key Concept & Highlight Extractor: Automatically extracts key concepts and highlights important keywords from processed documents or notes.
- AI Study Schedule Planner: Generates personalized study schedules, timetables, and daily challenges with integrated reminders for students.
- User Authentication & Profiles: Secure login for teachers and students, allowing access to their respective modules and saved content.
- Content Upload Interface: Enables teachers to provide topics/syllabus for quiz generation and students to upload study materials (PDF, PPTX, DOCX) for note and quiz generation.
- Teacher & Student Dashboards: Separate, intuitive dashboards for teachers to manage quizzes and students to access notes, schedules, and attempt quizzes.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep, knowledge-associated blue (#257EDC). It provides a professional and clear focal point against lighter elements.
- Background color: Very light blue-grey (#EEF3F7). Heavily desaturated from the primary hue, offering a clean, airy, and academic backdrop for content readability.
- Accent color: A vibrant purple (#5E4CE6). Analogous to the primary color, this bright hue stands out for important calls to action and highlights, ensuring clear visual hierarchy.
- Headlines font: 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif) for a modern, tech-informed, and slightly scientific feel.
- Body font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for excellent readability, a neutral, and objective appearance suitable for academic content.
- Utilize clear, minimalistic outline icons that communicate functionality quickly and maintain a clean academic aesthetic.
- Employ a clean, structured dashboard-style layout for both teacher and student modules, emphasizing hierarchical information and ease of navigation.
- Subtle and swift animations for content loading, generation feedback, and smooth transitions between views, enhancing user experience without distraction.
